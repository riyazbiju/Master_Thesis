\section{Contributions of the Thesis}\label{sec:intro_contrib}

This thesis makes the following contributions, corresponding to the
research questions introduced in Section~\ref{sec:intro_rq}.

\textbf{Knowledge integration and historical evidence retrieval.} In
response to RQ1, the thesis develops an approach for integrating
heterogeneous industrial quality information and retrieving relevant
historical evidence for newly recorded quality incidents. The approach
connects information from quality incidents, rework activities, inspection
results, and problem-solving records, including situations in which no
identical historical case exists.

\textbf{Multi-source evidence reasoning for causer attribution.} In
response to RQ2, the thesis develops a method for combining and evaluating
evidence from different historical sources. The method distinguishes
supporting, conflicting, and insufficient evidence and uses these signals
to recommend the most likely process-level causer of a quality incident.

\textbf{Explainable decision support for industrial users.} In response to
RQ3, the thesis develops an explainable decision-support framework that
presents the recommended causer together with the historical evidence
supporting it, relevant contradictions, recurring patterns, applicable
lessons learned, and the limitations of the recommendation.

The framework is implemented and evaluated using the distinction between
supplier-related and internally caused automotive quality incidents. The
first contribution addresses how relevant industrial knowledge can be
integrated and retrieved; the second addresses how the retrieved evidence
can be evaluated; and the third addresses how the resulting recommendation
can be explained and presented for human validation. This produces a clear
correspondence between the research questions and the contributions of
this thesis, summarized in Table~\ref{tab:rq_contrib}.
